# Self-contained S01

## Program frame (normative in this document)

Operon is a zero-permanent-UI, agent-agnostic operating kernel. One kernel owns versioned contracts, identity and authority, canonical state, proposals, execution receipts, evidence and the result algebra. CLI, MCP, SDK, generated application surfaces, recipes and enterprise modules are adapters over that kernel and may not reimplement its rules.

Release order is V0 -> V1 -> V2 -> V3. V0 proves external-agent authoring, ingestion, reconciliation and safe action. V1 proves Action Inbox, exact approval, durable execution and honest outcomes. V2 adds post-V0 missions, disposable generated surfaces and the recipes/skills/module ecosystem. V3 adds federation, factory, sovereign operations and formal assurance. A later release cannot be claimed before the preceding gate passes on the same pinned candidate with independent evidence.

Current status is `SPEC_CONSISTENT_NOT_PRODUCT_PASS`: no runtime case has been executed by this dossier.

## Decision capsule (normative in this document)

- D16: V0 is CLI/MCP-first, agent-agnostic and has no permanent UI; both public OSS and private real-company proof gates are mandatory.
- D-CONS-06: execute V0-A protocol/state, V0-B authoring, V0-C ingestion/provenance, V0-D reconciliation/mirror, V0-E safe use/action/views, then F1/F2.
- D-CONS-04: demo, maintenance, production and external-agent profiles are distinct; a client cannot mint identity or authority.
- D-CONS-01: canonical terms are TaskMandate, IntentGrant, WorldView, PreparedAction, DefinitionRelease and ChangeSet.
- D-CONS-02: SQLite is the durable local/benchmark profile; Node/PostgreSQL is the real-company reference profile; evidence never transfers between profiles.
- D15: V0 executes bounded registered actions only; larger plans and missions are post-V0.
- D05 engineering default: business results are exactly ALLOW, DENY, REVIEW_REQUIRED or EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT; infrastructure failure is a separate channel. Changing this requires a versioned decision and migration.
- D01-D04 are recipe-specific open decisions, never generic-kernel blockers. D-V0-BUSINESS-01 and D-V0-LICENSE-01 remain open and nonblocking until a commercial or public licensing claim is made. V0 OSS distribution excludes company data, hidden cases, gold outputs, private oracles, thresholds and evaluator weights.

## Purpose and baseline

Build one governed operational platform, not independently evolving engines with inconsistent authority. Retain the submitted TypeScript/Effect foundation. The candidate declares Effect `4.0.0-rc.112`, pnpm `10.4.1`, MCP SDK `^1.30.0` and Alchemy `2.0.0-beta.76`. These declarations are not an installation or compatibility result. Qualification must use the exact lockfile and source tree, then publish a resolved toolchain manifest.

## Normative candidate contract

Separate the trusted control plane, authoritative operational state, derived knowledge/indexes, untrusted execution and user-facing projections. These are logical boundaries; they do not require five microservices. The reference cell proposed here is Node/Effect with PostgreSQL and a single local unit of work. SQLite remains a separately qualified local profile. Cloudflare deployments need provider-specific contracts; SQL dialect generation is not provider certification.

Requests receive server-derived `ScopeContext`: tenant, ontology, execution environment, scenario or production identity, actor, policy epoch and correlation. Missing scope fails closed. Nested actions, scheduled jobs, caches and exports preserve scope. Trusted service composition selects live versus simulated adapters. A user-supplied `scenario=false` cannot select a privileged runtime.

All effectful work is lazy until run. Resources are acquired in scopes, closed on failure/interruption, and not retained across tests through mutable global singletons. Typed domain outcomes remain separate from unexpected defects, unavailable dependencies and timeouts. Caught exceptions must not manufacture a successful business outcome.

## Source precedence and change control

Preserve the original 247 requirement records, their 494 procedures, the 54 maximum-scope requirements and their original Portuguese wording, the additional 60 Better Auth cases, and every historical finding. New requirements are marked `CONSOLIDATION_PROPOSAL`. Neither source ID nor `extends` is automatically a scheduling dependency. This dossier proposes technical choices; it does not resolve the original 17 open source/product decisions or authorize a regulated workflow.

An ADR must identify the decision, options, chosen scope, limitations, owner and approval state. Changes to accepted source meaning require a new decision version and explicit test migration. A dependency upgrade changes the candidate and invalidates applicable evidence until rerun. Do not compare scenario totals as a percentage of production completeness.

## First executable checks

Clean extraction; input and per-file hashes; package-manager bootstrap; frozen-lock installation in a controlled worker; actual TypeScript build, Effect tests and CLI binary; packaged-consumer imports; zero skipped applicable tests. Missing registry access is `BLOCKED_ENVIRONMENT`, not an application failure or a pass. Parse-only and source-level results retain their narrower evidence class.

## Out of scope of a local qualification

A successful local cell says nothing by itself about Cloudflare persistence, cross-process locks, enterprise offboarding, model safety, production recovery or financial/quality-system suitability. Each advertised profile closes its own contracts.
