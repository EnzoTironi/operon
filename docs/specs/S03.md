# Self-contained S03

## Program frame (normative in this document)

Operon is a zero-permanent-UI, agent-agnostic operating kernel. One kernel owns versioned contracts, identity and authority, canonical state, proposals, execution receipts, evidence and the result algebra. CLI, MCP, SDK, generated application surfaces, recipes and enterprise modules are adapters over that kernel and may not reimplement its rules.

Release order is V0 -> V1 -> V2 -> V3. V0 proves external-agent authoring, ingestion, reconciliation and safe action. V1 proves Action Inbox, exact approval, durable execution and honest outcomes. V2 adds post-V0 missions, disposable generated surfaces and the recipes/skills/module ecosystem. V3 adds federation, factory, sovereign operations and formal assurance. A later release cannot be claimed before the preceding gate passes on the same pinned candidate with independent evidence.

Current status is `SPEC_CONSISTENT_NOT_PRODUCT_PASS`: no runtime case has been executed by this dossier.

## Data model
Retain immutable source envelopes: source system, external ID, version, content digest, received time, source effective time, author, classification, allowed purposes and deletion/retention policy. Store original bytes separately from extracted assertions. A `Claim` links subject/property/value or relationship to evidence and derivation records. Its lifecycle distinguishes proposed, supported, accepted, contested, superseded, retracted and unknown states; exact business interpretation remains versioned.

Maintain a canonical identity registry with source-system keys and reversible merge/split events. Structured IDs use deterministic mapping. Language-model matching may propose ambiguous associations but cannot merge authority or grant membership. Splitting two mistakenly merged customers preserves historical references and invalidates affected projections.

## Authority and conflicts
Authority is defined per field, source, scope and effective interval—not globally by connector or recency. A contractual deadline, a forecast and a requested deadline are different properties. Contradictory claims remain visible until a declared rule or authorized adjudication resolves them. Confidence scores are evidence about an inference, not permission to overwrite accepted state.

Admission writes use a protected workflow. Extractors, Graphiti and model workers emit candidates; they do not hold direct credentials to update authoritative records. Every accepted change links a decision, evidence closure and governing authority. A source correction updates the present view while preserving what was known before the correction.

## Completeness and derived data
`EvidenceClosure R(d)` names each required input, source revision, maximum permitted age and dependency predicate. Missing, hidden, unknown, inapplicable and stale are separate states. A privileged evaluator may use confidential evidence to reject a plan, but return only a disclosure-safe verdict. Restricted data is not equivalent to missing data.

Derived summaries, embeddings, indexes, observations, caches and model inputs inherit applicable use/disclosure restrictions. Retraction schedules invalidation across all downstream artifacts and pending decisions. Retention, deletion and legal hold conflicts require owner decisions; auditability is not permission to retain all original content indefinitely.

## Interfaces
`Ingest.accept(envelope)`, `Identity.proposeMatch`, `Identity.commitMerge`, `Claims.extract`, `Admission.evaluate`, `Admission.commit`, `Lineage.impact`, `Evidence.resolveClosure`. Each is environment-scoped and uses stable source-event idempotency. A replay must not create duplicate claims or recompute history under an unrecorded model revision.

## Acceptance
Duplicate and reordered source events; reversible entity resolution; conflicting deadlines; missing inspections; stale connector; evidence deletion; mixed-source private summaries; same external ID in two tenants; compromised source instructions. Positive controls show legitimate admission and correction work. A deleted source cannot keep authorizing cached approvals. Original source envelopes and executed extraction outputs support independent reconstruction.
